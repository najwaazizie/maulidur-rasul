Remembrance

Better manners

Prophetic etiquette encourages respectful behaviour toward family, friends and community.

💗

Kindness

Helping, smiling and speaking well make good character visible in everyday life.

Consistency

Small repeated actions can build discipline and intentional habits.

Helping people and removing harm show responsibility toward the community.
